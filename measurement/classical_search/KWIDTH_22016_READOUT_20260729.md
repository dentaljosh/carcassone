## 3. THE HEADLINE — the compound collapse

The mean alone is inconclusive. The **product** of the two things budget has to do to buy
strength — change the move, *and* change it for the better — is the decision-relevant
quantity, and both factors fell:

| | 2752 → 11008 (oracle pilot) | **11008 → 22016 (this run)** | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| disagreement rate D | 0.2398 | **0.1244 ± 0.0076** | **0.519×** |
| mean pts per disagreement | +0.7375 | **+0.1054** | **0.143×** |
| **⇒ pts per MOVE (D × mean)** | **+0.1769** | **+0.0131** | **0.074× = a 13.5× collapse** |

⚠️ **Read this as DESCRIPTIVE, not as a significance claim.** The compound's own interval,
D̂ × the CR CI, is **[−0.036, +0.062] — it includes zero**, because it inherits the mean's
inconclusiveness. What is *powered* here is the comparison, not the level:

- **The CI upper bound (+0.497) EXCLUDES the rung below's +0.7375.** So the genuinely
  powered statement this run supports is **"this step is measurably less valuable per
  disagreement than 2752 → 11008"** — not "this step is zero".
- **D̂'s halving is powered on its own** (±0.0076 on 1905 cells).

**Order-of-magnitude elo cross-check — a consistency test, NOT a measurement** (the same
non-additivity caveat as the pilot's §4 applies: swapping one decision per game does not
sum, and the naive sum overstates — measured at 3.2× on the rung below):

> 0.0131 pts/move × 71.5 decisions ≈ 0.94 pts/game naive → ÷3.2 non-additivity ≈ 0.29
> pts/game → wr 0.5053 (σ_game 22.2, [LUCK_FLOOR](../human_anchor/LUCK_FLOOR.md)) ≈ **+3.7 elo**.

Against `results.csv`: `curve_k16x1376_22016_vs_deploy_k4x688` (+35.58) minus
`cl060_h2h_k8x1376_vs_deploy_k4x688` (+49.85) = **−14.3 elo** by difference (cross-band,
n.s.). **+3.7 and −14.3 are both indistinguishable from zero**, so the two instruments now
agree that this rung buys ~nothing — which is the useful part, because they disagreed
sharply one rung down (+49.85 head-to-head vs −21.4 through RoD-v2).

## 4. ⚠️ SELF-CRITICISM: the prereg's KNEE branch was unreachable at the realized sd

This must be stated, because otherwise the "INCONCLUSIVE" verdict looks like a coin that
could have landed either way. It could not.

The prereg's knee branch required **95 % CI upper bound < +0.3688** (half the pilot's
effect). It planned that against the pilot's per-position **sd 2.406**. The realized sd is
**2.9495** — 23 % higher — so the CR CI half-width is **±0.3918**, *already larger than the
threshold itself*. The knee branch could therefore only have fired on a point estimate
**≤ −0.023**, i.e. only if the wider pick had come out nominally *worse*.

**⇒ The design could reach "there IS room" or "INCONCLUSIVE", but not "at the knee", at any
positive point estimate.** That is a power mis-specification in §5, not a property of the
world, and it is the single thing to fix if this is ever re-opened.

**Re-open bar, priced:** powering the knee branch *at the observed +0.1054* needs
se ≤ 0.1344 ⇒ **n ≈ 482 scored positions ⇒ ~3875 cells** ⇒ roughly **2× this run's
cost (~11 h at W16)**. Also available and cheaper: the sd is dominated by the
**between-position** term (var_between 3.81 vs within/M 4.88; sd at M=64 would still be
2.50, at M→∞ ≈ 1.95), so **more worlds cannot rescue it** — the same wall the pilot hit.

**Not recommended.** It would buy a tighter estimate of a quantity every reading already
puts near zero, and 22016 is not deployable regardless (§5).

## 5. What this changes, and what it does not

**Changes — the knee question, one rung up.** The pilot established that the flat elo curve
at 2752 → 11008 was **the ruler, not convergence**. This run finds **no such rescue at
11008 → 22016**: the pick changes half as often, and where it changes the improvement is not
detectable. Combined with §3's cross-check (both instruments now read ~0 for this rung), the
plateau `PRODUCTION.yaml` cited to justify choosing 11008 over 22016 **looks like a real
plateau at this rung, not an artifact** — which *retrospectively supports the promotion's
"cheapest point on the plateau" reasoning* on a ruler that has no opponent, no elo and no
bands. **That is the finding: the promotion's judgement call survives an independent check.**

**Does NOT change — anything deployable.** 22016 costs 2× the champion's clock. CL-068 and
[TOURNAMENT_TIMING](../../docs/research/TOURNAMENT_TIMING_2026-07-26.md) stand untouched; no
promotion candidate exists here and none is proposed. `PRODUCTION.yaml` untouched.

**Does NOT establish — that the search has converged.** Two independent reasons, both
pre-registered: (a) §4's judge bias runs toward zero, so a null under-states; (b) the pick
still changes **12.4 %** of the time, which is not convergence in CL-070's sense — it is
"changes, but the change is not worth anything measurable", the surviving hypothesis
CL-070's own correction flagged.

**Does NOT touch — the standing same-family caveat.** The judge is still a clairvoyant PUCT
search on the same curve125 leaf as the agents it judges. The pilot's out-of-family sign
check (§8 of [ORACLE_PILOT_EXT_READOUT](ORACLE_PILOT_EXT_READOUT_20260728.md)) covered the
rung below, not this one. It was **reserved as a follow-up and deliberately not run**; on a
null result it is also close to uninformative (there is no sign to check), which is itself a
reason not to spend on it.

**Reserved follow-ups, both still NOT RUN and neither recommended:** the `--oracle-sims 400`
sensitivity (addresses §4's weak-continuation bias — the *only* one that could overturn a
null, and the honest reason it is not free) and the out-of-family sign check.
