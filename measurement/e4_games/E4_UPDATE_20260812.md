## 5. Component ledger and the farm answer (C) — **the anomaly did not replicate**

This was the priority question and it has a clean answer.

| farm statistic | first 15 (prior standing) | **new 8** | Welch diff (new−old) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| paired farm margin | **+11.400 ± 3.588 (z +3.18)** | **+1.500 ± 3.929 (z +0.38)** | −9.900 ± 5.32, z **−1.86** |
| champion farm pts/seat | **14.000 ± 3.108** | **21.375 ± 2.803** | +7.375 ± 4.18, z +1.76 |
| human farm pts/seat | 25.400 ± 2.150 | 22.875 ± 2.333 | −2.525 ± 3.17, z −0.80 |
| champion held to **zero** farm pts | **4 of 15** | **0 of 8** | — |
| games where Joshua won the farm ledger | **12 W / 1 L / 2 tied** | **4 W / 4 L / 0 tied** | — |

**The champion's farm points against Joshua snapped back to its own corpus norm.** The whole
anomaly was "14.0 pts/seat against him vs 20.5 in its own corpus" (≈2.09σ low). In the 8 new
games it scores **21.375**, i.e. *at* the corpus norm, and it is never zeroed. Pooled over the
full epoch the paired margin is still +7.957 ± 2.839 (z +2.80), but that pool now averages a
strong first half with a null second half; the pooled z is a legacy of the first 15, not a
current property.

**What moved is the champion's farm score, not Joshua's** — his own farm points are flat
(25.4 → 22.9, z −0.80). So this is not "he stopped farming"; it is "the champion stopped being
shut out." That is consistent with his own account (`JOSHUA_PLAY_EVOLUTION.md`: *"I don't
commit to farms early. but I'll challenge his farms early if they look juicy"*) having been an
**early-epoch** description that no longer characterises the games — but the archives cannot
tell us why, only that the effect is gone.

**Where his edge went instead:** in the new 8 he is +6.750 on during play and +3.500 on
unfinished features and only +1.500 on farms. In the first 15 it was +1.667 / −3.933 / +11.400.
He is now winning the same amount of margin out of completely different components. Given a
fixed, non-adapting opponent and a self-reportedly adapting human, a shift in *which component*
the edge comes from is exactly what a strategy change looks like — and it is another reason not
to pool this epoch as if it were one population.

FARMWAR's closing line was *"the cheapest thing that would move it is more fixed_v1-epoch
games."* Eight arrived, and they moved it. **The farm ledger no longer wants an explanation of
the kind FARMWAR was chasing; the first independent test past n=15 came back null.**

## 7. What this does NOT say

- **It does not say the farm effect was never real.** It says it did not replicate on n=8, that
  the Welch contrast between halves is z −1.86 (itself only suggestive), and that the pooled
  z +2.80 is now carried entirely by the first half. Both "it was a n=15 fluctuation" and "it
  was real and he stopped doing it" fit these data. Nothing here distinguishes them.
- **It does not say Joshua is improving.** The trend is z +1.47 and its point estimate *halved*
  on new data. And the assists are unstamped, so a change in assistance is observationally
  identical to a change in skill.
- **It does not rate the champion or Joshua.** Unpaired, one seat, n=23, a non-stationary
  anchor, unstamped assists, and a documented ~6.25% luck floor. Phase-C sizing for a real read
  is still **193 seat-swap deck-paired games at true wr 0.55**; 23 unpaired games is not that,
  and 26 games at a 0.54 raw winrate is ~1.8σ from a coin.
- **It does not mint a claim, flip a governance row, or touch `PRODUCTION.yaml`.** The E4
  stream remains an out-of-lineage human *reference*, not a promotion instrument.
- **It does not say the app is fine end to end.** It says the archive schema is unchanged, the
  seed bug is behaviourally fixed, and 26/26 replay exactly. It also says the phone has
  silently stopped holding the two oldest archives, which nobody was watching for.
- **The grading section is incomplete** and no grading conclusion should be drawn from it until
  §6 is filled in.
