  red-team judge panel. Descriptive censuses on **banked data**; **zero games played**.

  kill, and the mechanism then still owes its own powered experiment.

## Standing guardrails inherited from the campaign

1. **CL-085 (judge-family relativity).** All three censuses are deliberately
   **judge-free**: they read the archive's realized actions, the engine's own feature
   decomposition, and the champion's own search statistics. No leaf-priced "headroom",
   no clairvoyant judge, no oracle scorer appears anywhere in this file. Nothing here
   is licensed to be re-read through a judge later and quoted as the same number.
2. **CL-084 (winner's curse).** No statistic in this file argmaxes over noisy arms and
   then quotes the winner's own-data gap. Census 1 reports the *rate* at which an
   alternative pooling rule changes a pick — it deliberately does **not** price the
   change, because pricing an argmax-selected change on its own selection data is the
   exact instrument CL-084 kills.
3. **Cross-band humility (CL-068).** No band pooling occurs; nothing here is a
   head-to-head and no elo is produced.
4. **Epoch conditioning (E4 archive rule).** Every E4-derived statistic is reported
   overall AND split by the archive's `rules_profile` epoch and by budget epoch
   (`result.budget_note` / `sims_effective` × `k_dets_effective`). The rules epoch is
   resolved from the archive's own `rules_profile`/`cloister_rule`/`farm_rule` fields,
   never from `(start_rule, grid_rule)`.

# CENSUS 1 — GT-M1 world-spread (CVaR / quantile pooling viability)

## The question

At contest-exposed plies, do the champion's **k = 8 PIMC determinization worlds already
agree on the root argmax**? If they do, then quantile / CVaR pooling over worlds is
**arithmetically inert** — it cannot change the move — and GT-M1 dies for free.

## The arithmetic lemma this census rests on (stated before compute)

Let `q_i(a)` be action *a*'s per-world Q in world *i*. If action `a*` satisfies
`q_i(a*) ≥ q_i(b)` for every world *i* and every alternative *b* (i.e. `a*` is the
per-world argmax in **all** worlds), then `a*` state-wise dominates every `b` across
the world distribution. CVaR at any level α is **monotone under state-wise dominance**,
so `CVaR_α(a*) ≥ CVaR_α(b)` for every α and every `b`. **Unanimity therefore implies
inertness for every world-monotone pooling rule** — mean (the deployed rule), any
quantile, any CVaR level. The converse does not hold, so unanimity is a *sufficient*
inertness test, not a necessary one. That is exactly why the CVaR reachability
computation below is PRIMARY and unanimity is its interpretable companion.

## Population

- **PRIMARY: the contest-exposed plies** = strata `invasion` ∪ `defense` ∪
  `farm_capture` of the 290-ply crux corpus, restricted to the `fixed_v1` epoch and
  after the shared exclusions (forced moves, exact-region roots).
- **COMPANION: the `control` stratum**, reported alongside, never pooled into the
  primary. Its purpose is to show whether contest exposure moves world spread at all.

## Definitions (fixed before compute)

- **Per-world Q.** For world *i*, `q_i(a) = W_i(a) / N_i(a)` from that world's
  `root_stats_list` (root-POV-signed W, deduped children), for actions with
  `N_i(a) ≥ 2` — the deployed `DEFAULT_MIN_POOLED_VISITS`, applied per world here. This
  per-world application of the pooled constant is a **disclosed choice**, not the
  deployed rule (the deployed rule applies it to the pool).
- **Per-world argmax** `a_i = argmax_a q_i(a)` over that world's eligible actions,
  ties broken by `(q, N, -action)` — the same key `pooled_q_argmax` uses.
- **Pooled argmax** `a* = pooled_q_argmax(agg_n, agg_w, 2)` over all 8 worlds — the
  literal deployed decision.
- **`agree_frac(ply)` = |{ i : a_i == a* }| / 8.**
- **`unanimous(ply)` = (agree_frac == 1.0)**; **`dissent(ply)` = (agree_frac < 1.0)**,
  i.e. at least one world names a different move.
- **CVaR-eligible action.** An action with `N_i(a) ≥ 2` in **all 8** worlds. An action
  the search barely looked at in some world has no defined per-world Q there and cannot
  be given one by a pooling rule. Per-ply eligible-action counts are disclosed.
- **CVaR pooling.** For a level α, `CVaR_α(a)` = the mean of the `ceil(8α)` **smallest**
  values of `{ q_i(a) }` (lower tail = the risk-averse / adversarial-world reading GT-M1
  proposes). **Pre-stated grid: α ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}** (α=1.00 is the mean =
  the deployed rule and serves as the arithmetic identity control: its pick MUST equal
  `a*` on the CVaR-eligible restriction, and any ply where it does not is flagged as an
  instrument fault, not a finding).
- **CVaR reach** at level α = the ply is counted as *reached* iff
  `argmax_a CVaR_α(a)` over CVaR-eligible actions ≠ `a*`.

## Statistics reported

1. **PRIMARY — `reach(α)`** = fraction of contest-exposed plies where CVaR pooling at
   level α changes the pick, for each α in the grid. Headline = `max_α reach(α)` over
   α ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75} (α=1.00 is the control).
2. **COMPANION — `U`** = fraction of contest-exposed plies that are unanimous.
3. Distribution of `agree_frac` (all 9 values 0/8 … 8/8), overall and per stratum.
4. Mean number of distinct per-world argmaxes per ply.
5. Salt-1 replicate of (1) and (2), reported as stability, never pooled.
6. `walled` + `app_aug2` leg, reported separately.

Cluster-robust binomial SEs by **game** are reported for `reach` and `U` for context
only; no bar below is a significance bar.

## KILL BARS (fixed before compute)

**PRIMARY (arithmetic reachability):**

- **GT-M1 IS KILLED** if `max_α reach(α) ≤ 0.10` over α ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75} on the
  contest-exposed population — no risk level in the grid can change the move on more
  than 1 ply in 10, so the entire lever is confined to ≤10% of the plies it was
  proposed for, before any question of whether the changed move is *better*.
- **GT-M1 SURVIVES THIS CENSUS** if `max_α reach(α) ≥ 0.30`.
- `0.10 < max_α reach(α) < 0.30` = **AMBER** — the census does not settle it; the
  number and the implied ply-share ceiling are reported and GT-M1 stays open.

**COMPANION (unanimity, the interpretable form of "already agree"):**

- **"already agree" is declared TRUE** if `U ≥ 0.80` — all 8 worlds name the pooled pick
  on ≥80% of contest-exposed plies. By the lemma above this alone caps every
  world-monotone pooling rule at <20% of plies.
- **"already agree" is declared FALSE** if `U ≤ 0.50`.
- In between: reported as-is, no label.

**Composite verdict rule:** GT-M1 is recorded **DEAD** if the primary bar fires.
If the primary is AMBER but `U ≥ 0.80`, the verdict is recorded as
**DEAD-ON-CEILING** (the lever is capped below the share that could plausibly matter)
with the reach number disclosed. Otherwise GT-M1 is **NOT KILLED**.

**Pre-committed non-inference.** Whatever `reach` comes out to, this census says
**nothing** about whether the CVaR pick is *better*. Any such claim requires
independent-world realized-outcome pricing (CL-084) and is out of scope here.

# CENSUS 2 — CF-M1 follow-through (setup-abandonment)

## The question

Does the champion **abandon its own setups** more than the owner does? This is the
behavioral signature the compute-first lens asked for. Pure archive read; **no bar
beyond direction** — the result licenses or kills the next CF gate.

## Population

All 61 archives in `measurement/e4_games/`, **both seats**. Owner = the archive's
`human_player`; champion = the other seat. Reported overall and split by rules epoch
and budget epoch.

## Definitions (fixed before compute — derived from the engine's own action encoding
and feature decomposition, exactly as `stage_a_census.py` does it)

- **Feature classes in scope: CITY and ROAD only.**
  - **Farms are EXCLUDED**: a farm has no "natural completion move" — it is scored only
    at game end, so "follow-through" is undefined for it.
  - **Cloisters are EXCLUDED**: a cloister is completed by *neighbouring* tile
    placements that are not directed extensions of a claimed component, and either
    player's tiles complete it, so abandonment is not attributable.
  - This exclusion is a disclosed narrowing of "setup", not a claim that farm play
    carries no setups.
- **SETUP EVENT.** At the meeple-phase ply *t* at which player *P* places a meeple that
  claims component *F* (class city or road), *F* is a setup iff *F* is **unfinished**
  at *t* (open edge count ≥ 1). Component identity follows `stage_a_census`'s union-find
  over `flat_leaf.decompose` reps, so a component that later merges is tracked through
  the merge.
- **FOLLOW-THROUGH WINDOW: N = 12 plies** after the claim ply. Rationale, fixed here:
  the archive alternates tile-ply / meeple-ply, so 12 plies ≈ 6 tile placements ≈ 3 of
  *P*'s own turns — long enough for a genuine intent to show, short enough that
  "abandonment" is not just "the game ended". **Robustness rungs N = 6 and N = 20 are
  declared NOW** and reported alongside; N = 12 is the primary and the others cannot be
  promoted over it after the fact.
- **OWN FOLLOW-THROUGH.** Within the window, at least one **tile ply whose actor is P**
  increases *F*'s component tile count. Attribution is exact: exactly one tile is placed
  per tile ply, so the growth is unambiguously that actor's.
- **ABANDONMENT.** A setup is *abandoned* iff **all** of:
  1. no own follow-through in the window;
  2. *F* is not finished at any point in the window (a completed feature needs no
     follow-through);
  3. *F* is still **live** at the end of the window — ≥ 1 open edge remaining (a feature
     that got walled off by geometry was not abandoned, it was foreclosed).
- **CENSORING.** A setup whose window would extend past the game's last ply is
  **dropped from the primary** and reported separately as a censored count. (Declared
  now precisely because late-game claims are the ones most likely to look "abandoned"
  for a reason that has nothing to do with intent.)
- **RATE.** `abandonment_rate(seat) = #abandoned / #eligible setups` for that seat.
- **CONTRAST.** `D = rate(champion) − rate(owner)`. **Direction only.** SEs are
  cluster-robust by GAME and are reported for context; there is no significance bar
  and no z-threshold licenses anything here.

## Companion statistics (declared now)

- Mean own-growth events per setup within the window.
- **Opponent-growth rate** in the same window — the *extendability control*: it shows
  the feature was in fact reachable by somebody, so a high abandonment rate is not just
  "no legal extension existed".
- Finished-in-window rate (setups that completed and so never needed follow-through).
- Median `held_plies` and `held_to_end` rate per seat, for continuity with the Stage-A
  `commit` rows.
- Breakdown by class (city vs road) and by `size_at_claim`.

## READ RULE (fixed before compute)

- **CF-M1 IS KILLED** if `D ≤ 0` — the champion abandons its own setups **no more** than
  the owner does, so "the champion abandons setups" has no behavioral signature in the
  E4 record and cannot be the mechanism behind the owner's edge.
- **CF-M1 IS NOT KILLED** if `D > 0` — the signature exists in the archive and the next
  CF gate is licensed to price it. Magnitude and sign are reported; the census does
  **not** get to claim the abandonment *causes* the point gap.
- A `D > 0` that is entirely carried by one epoch, one feature class, or fewer than 20
  eligible setups on the champion side is reported as **NOT KILLED (FRAGILE)** with the
  carrying slice named.

# CENSUS 3 — SA-M1 contested-seed reachability

## The question

At the crux plies, does a **contested seed** — an action that starts or joins a
contested feature — **exist** among the legal actions at all, and if it does, does the
champion's search at deploy budget **already** give it real visits? If most crux plies
have no seed to reserve for, SA-M1 dies. If the seed is already well-funded by the
search, reservation buys nothing and SA-M1 dies. Either branch is a free kill.

## Population

The 290-ply crux corpus (same shared exclusions, same `fixed_v1` primary / other-profile
companion split as Census 1). The champion's deploy search is run **at the root
regardless of which seat actually moved there**, because SA-M1 is a property of the
champion's search, not of the realized game; the actor split is reported.

**CL-070's 898-root set** is addressed in `READOUT.md` §0 as a data-availability
question, decided before any of the numbers below were computed.

## Definitions (fixed before compute — aligned to Stage-A's contest semantics)

- **Both-player component.** A connected component of `flat_leaf.decompose` (city, road
  or farm) carrying at least one meeple of **each** player. This is exactly Stage-A's
  "contested" predicate.
- **CONTESTED SEED ACTION.** A legal action *a* at the root is tagged a contested seed
  iff, applying *a* to the root state, either:
  - **`onset`** — a component that is a both-player component *after* *a* was not one
    *before* (Stage-A's contest onset: `merge` / `born_contested` / claim); or
  - **`extend`** — *a* increases the tile count of a component that was **already** a
    both-player component before *a*.
  **PRIMARY tag set = `onset` ∪ `extend`.** `onset` alone is reported separately, since
  it is the stricter reading of "starting a contest".
- **EXISTS(ply)** = at least one legal action at that root carries the primary tag.
- **Visit adequacy.** From the SAME regenerated pooled root statistics as Census 1
  (k8 × 1376, pooled `agg_n` over the 8 worlds), `N_seed(ply)` = the **maximum** pooled
  visit count over the tagged contested-seed actions at that ply.
- **m — the "already well-visited" threshold — is fixed at m = 110 pooled visits**,
  i.e. **1% of the 11008-sim deploy budget**. Rationale, stated in advance: a budget
  *reservation* scheme exists to guarantee an arm a floor of attention. An arm the
  unmodified search already gives ≥1% of total budget is already above any floor a
  reservation would plausibly install, so reserving for it is a no-op. m is pre-stated
  as a share of budget, not as a raw number tuned to the data.

## Statistics reported

1. `P(EXISTS)` over the population, and per stratum.
2. Among plies with `EXISTS`: the distribution of `N_seed`, the fraction with
   `N_seed ≥ m`, the **pooled-visit rank** of the best contested seed among all visited
   root actions, and `N_seed / Σ_a agg_n(a)` as a budget share.
3. The **compound reachable share**: fraction of ALL population plies where a seed
   exists AND is under-visited (`N_seed < m`) — the only plies a reservation could
   possibly change.
4. `onset`-only versions of 1–3.
5. Mean count of tagged seeds per ply; mean `n_legal`.
6. Salt-1 replicate of 2–3 as stability; other-profile leg reported separately.

## KILL BARS (fixed before compute)

- **BRANCH A — nothing to reserve for.** SA-M1 **IS KILLED** if `P(EXISTS) < 0.50` —
  a majority of crux plies offer no contested seed at all, so a reservation has no
  target on most of the plies it was proposed for.
- **BRANCH B — already funded.** SA-M1 **IS KILLED** if, among plies with `EXISTS`,
  `P(N_seed ≥ m | EXISTS) ≥ 0.80` — on 4 in 5 of the plies that do have a seed, the
  unmodified search already funds it above the reservation floor, so reservation is a
  no-op there.
- **COMPOUND BRANCH.** SA-M1 **IS KILLED** if the compound reachable share (statistic 3)
  `≤ 0.20`, whether or not either single branch fires — the lever cannot touch 4 crux
  plies in 5.
- **SA-M1 SURVIVES THIS CENSUS** if none of the three branches fires. Surviving means
  only that the lever has a target with headroom; it says nothing about whether
  spending budget there would help.

**Pre-committed non-inference.** A high `N_seed` does not mean the seed is a *good*
move, and a low one does not mean it is a good move the search is missing. This census
measures **reachability of the lever**, not the value of the action. Pricing the action
would require independent-world outcome pricing and is out of scope.

## Instrument-fault triggers (any one voids the affected census)

- Census 1: the α = 1.00 CVaR control disagrees with `pooled_q_argmax` on any ply
  (restricted to CVaR-eligible actions).
- Censuses 1 & 3: any root whose replayed board string does not match the archive's
  own reconstruction, or whose replay raises.
- Census 2: any archive whose replayed final scores do not match the archive's
  recorded scores (`recon_ok` in the Stage-A sense).
- Any leg whose resolved leaf does not hash to `a36d2e15a3b3d71d`.
- Any leg whose `CARCASSONNE_FIX_R9` latch does not match its profile's
  `r9_env_expected`.

Fault counts are reported even when zero.
