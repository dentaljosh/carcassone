## Cross-cutting caveats every claim inherits
- **Clairvoyant** — all elo/solver labels assume the true future deck order (gap bounded small by CL-022, but the *preferred* fair-information ground truth is untested at K≥3).
- **In-ecosystem** — the ruler is the v2.7-leaf family, **not** human-anchored. No claim here supports an absolute "superhuman" or "human-expert" statement.
- **n=400 paired ≈ ±17.5 elo (1σ).** Cross-band composition stacks noise; only same-band paired comparisons compose. A lone >1σ spike vs neighbors is treated as noise, not signal.
- **Endgame-optimality ≠ full-game Elo** — kept deliberately separate (iter8 wins full games while being the least endgame-precise).
