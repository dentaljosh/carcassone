## sigma

`sigma` is the standard error of the elo estimate from the binomial score
`s=(W+0.5D)/n`: `SE(elo)=(400/ln10)·sqrt(s(1-s)/n)/(s(1-s))`. Near s=0.5 this
equals the `400/sqrt(n)` rule of thumb (≈20 at n=400, ≈40 at n=100). At n=400 a
result needs |elo| > ~35 to clear 2σ.

## CONTRADICTIONS — same config, different result (the whole point)

`build_results.py` auto-detects these: it groups every row by its full
(new-config vs old-config) signature, flags any signature measured 2+ times with
>20 elo **point-spread**, and reports the **pooled-σ separation** so we can tell a
real contradiction from a wide-but-consistent spread. The >20 point-spread is a
*screen for re-measurement candidates*, NOT proof of inconsistency. Two
signatures flagged: **one mild tension (1.4σ), one actually consistent (0.85σ)**,
plus one config-dependent flip on the same checkpoint.

> **Correction (provenance):** the first backfill pass had an elo bug — promoted
> Optuna trials paired their n=400 W/L/D with the n=100 *screen* elo (e.g. #5
> showed +20.9, #8 showed +56.1). Fixed by deriving elo from each row's own
> W/L/D (`elo_from_wld`). Corrected: #5 +12.2, #8 +12.2, #9 +24.4 (the real study
> best), #13 +18.3. Numbers below are post-fix.

### 1. (c=3.0, cap=12, v2_7) NEW vs (c=1.5, cap=12, v2_7) OLD — iter_B1 both sides

| exp | n | elo | sigma |
|---|---|---|---|
| `phase2_puct_c30_vs_c15` (2026-05-26) | 400 | **+47.2** | 17.5 |
| `optuna_trial_0017` (2026-05-28) | 100 | **+13.9** | 34.8 |

**Pooled separation: 0.85σ → CONSISTENT, not a statistical contradiction.** The
n=100 re-screen's error bars are wide enough that +13.9 and +47.2 agree within
noise — a single n=100 screen cannot refute the n=400 reading. So the table does
NOT prove +47.2 was wrong. The real reason to distrust +47.2 as a *standout* is
the **shape of the c-curve**, not this pair: in the Phase-2b sweep the neighbors
were c=2.5→+7.8 and c=4.0→+25.2, so +47.2 is a jagged spike sitting ~2× above
both sides — the signature of an upward sampling fluctuation on an otherwise flat
c∈[2,5] plateau. **Production default is still c=3 on that possibly-inflated
point.** Clean resolution: a fresh n=400 at exactly this signature (and ideally
re-measure the c=2.5/4.0 neighbors) to see if the spike survives.
